# Academic project briefs

Project proposals offered to universities and their students — written to be handed to a supervisor
as-is.

These are **genuine gaps**: things that do not exist publicly and that we think are worth building.
None of them sit on pAIchart's delivery path, so no student is working against our deadlines.

## Terms

- **No IP assignment, no exclusivity, no deliverable owed to us.** Publish it, keep the copyright,
  license it however your institution prefers.
- **Support is real, not nominal** — specifications, working lab environments, a platform account for
  end-to-end testing, and technical review at each milestone from the engineers who built the
  platform side.
- Where a brief overlaps something we are also building, it says so plainly.

## Enquiries

Via the repository, or the contact route named in the brief. We are happy to talk to a supervisor
before a student commits, and to adjust scope to fit a particular programme — a research masters and a
coursework capstone want different shapes, and the briefs note where that changes things.
