Objective. The pods of one Kubernetes namespace must be able to write to a cloud log-archive bucket, and nothing else may. Produce the requirements document for a program that restricts writes to that bucket to that namespace's pods, and that authorises exactly what the design decisions declare - nothing wider than them.

This is a DRAFT SPECIFICATION for human review. Do not launch a program.

- cloud   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paichart/paichart/main/descriptors/terraform-readonly-descriptor.json
